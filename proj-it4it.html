Overview

Making the complex comprehensible

To support the launch and adoption of The Open Group's IT4IT™ initiative, I led the creation of a series of explainer animations. The IT4IT™ standard defines a reference architecture for managing the business of IT — important, but inherently abstract. The brief was to make these concepts immediately understandable for IT leaders and decision-makers.

Acting as Creative Lead, I owned the project from the earliest stakeholder conversations through to final delivery — defining the narrative, developing the visual language, and guiding an external production agency through every stage of the work.

Creative Process

From brief to finished film

Three distinct phases took the project from an abstract standard to a polished animation series.

01

Stakeholder Discovery

  • Facilitated workshops with subject matter experts and leadership to surface objectives and key messages
  • Identified the core concepts that needed communicating — and the ones that didn't
  • Aligned stakeholders on tone, audience, and the level of technical detail appropriate for each animation
  • Defined success criteria and key deliverables before a single frame was drawn
02

Script & Storyboard

  • Wrote clear, accessible scripts — translating standards language into plain English without losing accuracy
  • Developed full storyboards, mapping the visual narrative beat by beat before production began
  • Iterated with stakeholders to refine pacing, messaging, and visual structure
  • Created a consistent motion language and style guide for the full series
03

Agency Production

  • Briefed and onboarded an external animation studio, providing all creative direction and approved assets
  • Managed ongoing feedback loops — reviewing cuts, aligning on revisions, and maintaining quality across the series
  • Ensured all animations remained true to the IT4IT™ brand and Open Group identity guidelines
  • Delivered finished MP4s and web-optimised versions for use across events, website, and presentations
