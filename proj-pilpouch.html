Overview

A medication management product built for real life — not the dispensary shelf.

PilPouch is a product developed by NuPAC, a PHOENIX company, as a more practical and versatile alternative to the standard MDS trays used in medication management. Rather than the clinical format of traditional trays, PilPouch uses tear-off pouches that each contain a patient's timed dose, labelled with the day and time they're due — simple enough to take away for a weekend, clear enough for patients to self-manage confidently.

I was brought in to design the packaging and promotional collateral, first launching under trial as a Rowlands Pharmacy branded product. Following a focused drive to bring the service to other pharmacies and medication providers, I then developed an unbranded refresh — one that sits comfortably in a patient's home and allows partnering clients to personalise it with their own branding.

Product Packaging

At home, not in the clinic

The unbranded NuPAC refresh was designed to feel approachable and domestic — a product that wouldn't look out of place on a kitchen counter. The distinctive teal and white colourway communicates cleanliness and trust without the cold detachment of traditional pharmaceutical packaging. The logo lockup leads with warmth: "your medication made easy."

Pouch Design

Tear off and go

Each pouch carries a single timed dose, clearly labelled with the day and time it should be taken. The perforated strip design makes it straightforward to tear off the doses you need — heading away for the weekend? Simply pull off Saturday and Sunday's pouches and you're set. The "tear open" indicator on each pouch keeps the experience intuitive even for patients managing their own medication for the first time.

Key Design Feature

Clear. Portable. Foolproof.

Perforated pouches allow individual doses to be detached cleanly from the strip. Each pouch shows the day, session (morning/evening), patient name and medication list — everything needed to self-administer with confidence, without relying on memory or a separate pill box.

Promotional Collateral

Selling the service to pharmacies

As NuPAC focused its commercial efforts on winning pharmacy and medication provider partners, a suite of promotional materials was developed to communicate the PilPouch proposition. The collateral spans brochure pages, case studies, and sales materials — all carrying the teal brand language and positioning PilPouch as a credible, scalable managed medication solution.

Process

From trial to scalable product

The project evolved in two distinct phases — a branded trial under Rowlands Pharmacy, followed by a standalone unbranded identity ready for broader commercial roll-out.

01

Brief & Product Understanding

Getting under the skin of what made PilPouch different — the advantages over MDS trays, the patient journey, and what trust and ease needed to look like in a domestic context.

  • Stakeholder briefing with NuPAC team
  • Review of existing MDS tray formats and competitor landscape
  • Defining tone: warm, trustworthy, non-clinical
02

Rowlands Pharmacy Edition

The first packaging range was developed as a Rowlands Pharmacy branded product for use during the trial period — working within the Rowlands brand guidelines while communicating the PilPouch product clearly.

  • Packaging dieline design and artwork
  • Rowlands brand application across packaging
  • Print-ready file preparation for litho production
03

NuPAC Unbranded Refresh

Following the trial, PilPouch received its own standalone identity — unbranded and designed for versatility, so partnering pharmacies and medication providers could personalise it with their own marque.

  • New packaging range designed from the ground up
  • Promotional brochure and sales collateral suite
  • Flexible brand system for client personalisation
