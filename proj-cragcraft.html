Overview

A brand built on the truth of the material

CragCraft needed a brand that was as honest as the product — no artifice, no imitation. Every hold is sourced from real rock: found in the field, cleaned, one side flattened to sit flush on a board, live edges left exactly as nature shaped them. The brand had to communicate that authenticity without over-explaining it.

The topo mark — five organic contour rings drawing inward to a summit point — became the central brand device, referencing both topographic maps and the natural formation of rock. Warm stone tones. Honest language. No resin. No moulds.

Brand Assets

Identity system

Strategy

90-day go-to-market plan

The launch strategy divides into three phases — each with a distinct focus, a clear set of actions, and measurable milestones. The goal: build a community before a catalogue, earn trust before revenue.

Phase 01

Days
1–30

Foundation & Community Seeding

  • Launch "Notify Me" landing page with two-stage email capture and audience segmentation from day one
  • Seed content documenting the rock sourcing and casting story — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit
  • Build credibility through authentic participation in r/climbharder and Mountain Project forums before any product mention
  • Outreach to 20 climbing gyms, 15 independent setters, and 10 micro-influencers for early access and feedback
  • Develop the Founding Collection: 8–10 SKUs named after geology — Sandstone Crimp, Granite Jug, Basalt Sloper

Target: 500 email subscribers

Phase 02

Days
31–60

Founding Collection Launch

  • Soft launch to email list Tuesday 5 AM, public launch across all channels Wednesday 10 AM
  • Coordinated drop across Instagram (3-post carousel), TikTok (2 launch videos), YouTube reveal, and email to 500 subscribers
  • Reddit post in r/climbharder framed as honest story — not a sales pitch
  • Activate paid media for the first time — retargeting landing page visitors and lookalike audiences
  • Collect reviews and real climber testimonials from beta testers to build social proof through the launch window

Target: £15,000 revenue in first 30 days

Phase 03

Days
61–90

Growth & Second Collection Prep

  • Establish repeatable content systems and weekly editorial rhythm across all channels
  • Formalise climbing gym and setter partnership programme — founding collection at 40% discount for staff testing
  • Introduce referral loop: each customer receives a unique referral link; first referral earns entry to next collection giveaway
  • Begin R&D and photography for Collection 2 — expand range, introduce new rock types and hold categories
  • Analyse data from launch window to optimise product mix, pricing, and channel allocation going forward

Target: 1,500 engaged community followers
