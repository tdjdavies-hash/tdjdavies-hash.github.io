Overview

The Open Group required a comprehensive brand refresh to modernize their visual identity while maintaining recognition within the enterprise architecture community. The project encompassed logo redesign, colour system development, typography refinement, and a complete design system.

System Applications

The brand system was applied across multiple touchpoints including corporate materials, digital platforms, and environmental design. Each application maintained consistency while adapting to specific contexts.

Result

A comprehensive, modern brand identity that strengthens The Open Group's presence in the enterprise architecture space while providing flexibility for future growth and applications.
