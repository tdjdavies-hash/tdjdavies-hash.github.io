Rare access to the world's leading minds in enterprise architecture

The Open Group hosts quarterly global summits bringing together the world's foremost practitioners in digital standards, enterprise architecture and IT governance. These events present a rare — and time-pressured — opportunity to capture video content with people who are world-class in their field but not always at ease in front of a camera.

01

Pre-production

Building the foundation

The work begins long before the event. Many speakers are world-leading academics and practitioners — not media personalities. Getting the best from them requires patience and preparation.

Identifying and contacting speakers weeks in advance
Building personal relationships and trust with subjects
Defining talking points tailored to each speaker's comfort level and expertise
Coordinating schedules around packed event programmes
Briefing the on-site videography team
02

Production

On location at the event

Shoot days at global summits are fast-moving. Speakers fit recordings around keynotes, workshops and networking — so every minute counts and setups need to work first time.

Directing videographers and managing on-site logistics
Setting up and striking portable interview rigs between sessions
On-camera directing — coaching nervous or camera-shy subjects
Capturing B-roll of the event floor, keynotes and breakout sessions
Managing shoots across multiple locations and time zones
03

Post-production

From rushes to final delivery

Post production runs to tight deadlines — content often needs to be live within days of an event to capitalise on momentum while speakers and audiences are still engaged.

Managing and reviewing rushes from videographers
Briefing and overseeing video editors
Managing review rounds with speakers and stakeholders
Delivering formatted outputs for YouTube, social and presentations
Meeting publication deadlines tied to event timelines

Overview

The full picture behind the lens

Producing video content at The Open Group's global events demands a different kind of producer — one who can earn the trust of world-class academics and practitioners, translate complex ideas into engaging on-camera conversations, and deliver polished content under the pressure of live event schedules. My role spans the entire production pipeline: from making first contact with speakers and shaping their talking points weeks before an event, to directing on location and managing post-production through to final delivery. The result is a growing library of thought leadership content that extends the reach of each event far beyond the room.
