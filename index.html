About

I've been working in design since 2013. I started out at Halton Print, a local print shop, designing everything from signs to van graphics, and occasionally found myself quoting jobs at the counter.

Since then I've worked in healthcare at PHOENIX Group, in social care at Community Integrated Care and in technology at The Open Group, picking up a much wider mix of creative work along the way. These days that means brand identities, campaigns, video and digital content, often taking a project from the first idea right through to the finished piece. In 2023 that included 32 videos with Forum Directors and executives, filmed in three days.

I enjoy getting stuck into the thinking behind a project: what needs to be said, who it's for and how to make it interesting. I like being involved the whole way through. Bouncing ideas around with people and seeing where they go is the best bit.

Outside of work, life is pretty busy with my partner and our four kids. When I do get some free time, you'll usually find me in the mountains climbing or hiking. Rainy days are for playing music.
