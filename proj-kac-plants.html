KAC Exotic
Plant Collective

Brand system and digital design for a rare houseplant shop. KAC came to me with a name, a logo and a library of plant photos. My job was to turn those into a brand that looked the same wherever it showed up, from the website to Instagram.

Client

KAC Exotic Plant Collective

Year

2025

What I did

Brand system, website and social design

The thinking

Rare plants make people nervous

Most plant shops sell on choice and price. KAC sold things like a variegated Monstera Thai Constellation or a Caladium 'Florida Moonlight', plants that are rare, often expensive and usually a bit fussy. What its customers wanted most was to feel sure they wouldn't kill them.

That's what I designed for. The brand needed to feel calm and knowledgeable, like buying from a friend who really knows their stuff.

The system

Built around an existing logo

I didn't design the logo, but I built everything around it. It's a monstera leaf breaking out of a square frame, with gold brush strokes behind it. I pulled a palette from the plants and their pots: terracotta, a dusky purple from the variegated leaves and a deep forest green. I chose Josefin Sans for headings, buttons and labels, because its clean geometric shapes sit well next to the hand-painted leaf.

Then I set the rules for using it, so the shop and the social posts felt like they'd come from the same place.

The shop

A shop as calm as the plants

Cream backgrounds, lots of space, big photos. Care guides sat in the main menu right next to the shop, because helping people keep their plants alive was part of what they were paying for.

The voice was soft and practical. It talked to people like a friend who knows plants, and it didn't hide which ones were tricky.
